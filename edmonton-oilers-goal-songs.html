Edmonton Oilers Goal Song

The current goal song for the NHL's Edmonton Oilers is "Striking Oil" by Royal Tusk.

Goal Song History

The Edmonton Oilers have used the following goal songs:

  • "Fluxland" by XL
  • "Stadium Love" by Metric
  • "Get Out of the Way" by Econoline Crush
  • "Don't Stop The Party" by Pitbull
