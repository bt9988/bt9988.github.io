Winnipeg Jets Goal Songs

The NHL's Winnipeg Jets currently play "Gonna Celebrate" by The Phantoms followed by individual goal songs afterwards.

Goal Song History

The Winnipeg Jets have used the following goal songs:

  • "Jump" by Van Halen (2016-2017)
  • "Shout" by The Isley Brothers (2015-2016)
  • "Hell Yeah" by Rev Theory (2011-2015)
