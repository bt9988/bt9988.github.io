Calgary Flames Goal Song

The current goal song for the NHL's Calgary Flames is "TNT" by AC/DC. This goal song has been in use since 2015.

Goal Song History

The Calgary Flames have used the following goal songs:

  • "Rock and Roll, Part 2" by Gary Glitter
  • "Disco Inferno" by The Trammps
  • "Shot Down In Flames" by AC/DC
  • "Barbra Streisand" by Duck Sauce
  • "Righteous Smoke" by Monster Truck
