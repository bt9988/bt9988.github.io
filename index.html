Welcome to Hockey Goal Songs, your guide to the current and past goal songs of NHL teams. Explore what each team plays after scoring, listen to the songs, and discover the history behind some of hockey's most recognizable goal anthems.

About Hockey Goal Songs

Hockey Goal Songs tracks the music NHL teams use to celebrate goals, from the songs currently played in arenas to goal songs from seasons past.

Select a team above to find its current goal song, listen to the track, watch it in action, and explore the team's goal song history.

Goal songs can change from season to season, so this site is continually updated as teams introduce new songs or bring back old favourites. If you notice something that needs updating, let us know.
