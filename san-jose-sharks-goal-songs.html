San Jose Sharks Goal Song

The current goal song for the NHL's San Jose Sharks is "Holiday" by Green Day.

Goal Song History

The San Jose Sharks have used the following goal songs:

  • "Power Bite" by BVRNOUT
  • "Get Ready for This" by 2 Unlimited (2016-2025)
  • "Let’s Go" by Sixx: A.M. (2015-2016)
  • "Holiday" by Green Day (2005-2006)
  • "Rock and Roll, Part 2" by Gary Glitter (2001-2005, 2006-2015)
